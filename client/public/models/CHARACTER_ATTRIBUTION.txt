CURRENT RUNTIME CHARACTERS — ORIGINAL BLENDER CAST

All active people use /models/animated/*.glb: attendant, attendant-player,
pilot, marshal, engineer and passenger-a/b/c. These are original FLIGHT 404
models built from geometry in scripts/blender/build_animated_crew.py,
with original materials, skeletons and sixteen action clips per character.
scripts/blender/add_safety_demonstrations.py adds SafetySeatbelt, SafetyOxygen,
SafetyLifeVest and SafetyExits to the twelve original movement/action clips.
The sidearm and five /models/safety/ equipment props are original separate
assets. They do not derive from the third-party scans listed below.
Editable base: models/blender/animated-crew.blend.
Current cast and safety equipment: models/blender/animated-crew-safety.blend.
Current hashes: models/blender/safety-demonstration-manifest.json.
